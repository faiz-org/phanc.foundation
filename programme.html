What We Do

PHANC Foundation · A4Apple Preschool

Our preschool programme

Learning that prepares children for life and school.

We connect learning to everyday experiences, giving children opportunities to explore, practise, ask questions and learn through interaction.

01

Language & communication

Stories, conversations, listening and speaking help children share ideas and express themselves.

02

Early literacy & numeracy

Age-appropriate activities introduce letters, sounds, numbers, shapes, patterns and quantities.

03

Thinking & problem-solving

Children observe, compare, sort, experiment, ask questions and work through simple challenges.

04

Social & emotional growth

Group activities help children practise sharing, cooperation, friendship and emotional expression.

05

Physical & creative development

Movement, drawing, painting and hands-on exploration support coordination and creativity.

06

School readiness

Classroom routines and group learning help children build confidence for the transition to formal schooling.

Learning rooted in community

Familiar experiences. Meaningful learning.

Children learn more meaningfully when ideas connect with their own lives. A4Apple adapts learning to the local context and uses child-friendly teaching-learning materials to make concepts tangible and engaging.

Our approach aims to be local and relatable, interactive, personalised and confidence-building. Educators make additional efforts to help children participate and learn at their own pace.

Play. Discover. Grow.Small steps make a difference.
